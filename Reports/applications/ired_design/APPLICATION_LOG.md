# IRED design application log

This log is separate from `PROJECT_LOG.md`; nothing recorded here changes CROSSFIT.

- [DECISION] The application reuses frozen architecture and hyperparameter selections but retrains generation models on all 1,182 curated non-WT variants. No application result is a CROSSFIT gate, table, or claim.
- [DECISION] Complete stage manifests are written only after expected artifacts validate. Interrupted shards resume only when their checkpoint and row-count contract both pass; otherwise that shard restarts from its beginning.
- [CAVEAT] Models predict campaign fitness, a selection readout. On external plate kcat, classical encodings transferred at Spearman 0.53-0.57 while ESM arms transferred at 0.23-0.44; classical arms therefore have stronger evidence when catalytic rate is the goal.
- [DECISION] The completed launch's representative benchmark projects 5.44 GPU-hours for the unreduced 500-winner/50-survivor search, inside the registered 3-6 GPU-hour estimate. The full pool and survivor counts are therefore retained. An earlier failed launch produced a slower benchmark and proposed 300/30, but stopped before predictions and that provisional reduction never governed a valid result.
- [ADJUST] The full-pool round-2 realization showed that the representative benchmark omitted material CPU/head and control-audit wall time; extrapolation crossed the six-hour ceiling. The run was stopped before any round-2 shard was written. Its 52 complete round-1 shards were archived, and the registered budget fallback of 300 winners and 30 survivors was activated. Complete single-scan predictions and the controls audit are reused; only their selection flags/pools are recomputed.
- [ADJUST] The first search launch stopped before producing any prediction artifact because the application loader treated the Step 4 JSON manifest as a YAML project config. The loader now reads JSON directly, and the search restarted from the beginning.
- [ADJUST] The second search launch stopped before producing any prediction artifact because the application phi generation head had been fitted to all 960 dictionary columns while the frozen selected Step 9 arm uses the 642 live columns. The five phi members were retrained from the beginning with the frozen live-feature mask; all now record input shape 642. No invalid prediction was retained.
- [DECISION] ThermoMPNN is the stability oracle. Its deposited-chain sequence matches the 290-residue campaign wild type exactly after accounting for the oracle's zero-based position column. Candidate multi-mutant values are explicitly additive compositions of single-substitution estimates, not direct epistatic predictions.
- [FINDING] Final cross-model consensus is weak: the maximum is 2 of 13 models, and all 30 reported candidates are shared only by the paired one-hot/AAIndex ridge arms. Because these linear encodings span nearly the same space, this is not independent architecture-family agreement.
- [FINDING] The controls expose known false positives. A72T ranks 2-3 for the four classical ridge arms, and G37C ranks 6 for both unaugmented classical arms. H244Y ranges from rank 8 to 3,898 across models. The individual ranks are retained rather than averaged away.
- [FINDING] From S68P-T241A, both unaugmented classical arms traverse both third founders in round 1 and the campaign peak in round 2, but they also select both known dead ends in round 1. The random forest reaches only the P203Q third founder; the remaining ten arms do not recover a registered winning node.
- [FINDING] Across all model/founder/round top lists, 45 selected rows match measured campaign variants. Per-model recovery ranges from 11 for each unaugmented classical ridge to zero for adapter, phi, ESM-mean ridge, ESM-mutpool ridge, and ESM-residue conv.
- [FINDING] Existing Mondrian calibration is uninformative for the final 6-8-mutation designs: supported six-mutation strata contain one calibration row and yield an unbounded 90% interval; seven- and eight-mutation strata are absent. Phi and adapter have no registered Step 2 conformal calibrator.
- [COST] Accounted application GPU wall time is 5.719 hours against the six-hour ceiling, including generation fitting, failed pre-result launches, the archived full-pool attempt, the valid reduced search, and two final uncertainty passes (the second restored uncertainty columns after correcting the trajectory manifest).
